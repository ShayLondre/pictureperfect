Photos that look blurry, too dark, overexposed, or are weaker shots of the same moment are marked Delete. Click the label to change it, or click a photo to see it large and use K keep, D delete, 1–5 stars, arrows to move.

Deleted photos go to the Mac Trash, so you can still get one back with Put Back until you empty the Trash.

Recognize faces
I'll find the faces in your photos and group the ones that look like the same person. You name each group once, and every photo of them gets tagged. Everything stays on this Mac. It needs a one-time download of about 15 MB, and the first pass over a big library can take a few hours — it runs in the background.
